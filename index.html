How to Copy Your PowerSchool Schedule

In PowerSchool, your schedule is organized with the Course Name, Bell/Period designation, and Teacher Email link. Follow the visual steps below:

Step 1: Open PowerSchool -> Click "My Schedule" -> Matrix or Table View Step 2: Start your mouse selection at the top course name. Step 3: Drag diagonally across all rows down to the last Teacher Email.
┌── [CLICK & DRAG START HERE] ───────────────────────────────┐ │ English 9 1(M,W-F) │ │ Room 214 Rm: 214 │ │ Email Smith, J. - Rm: 214 │ │ │ │ Chemistry - AEDT 2(M-W,F) │ │ Room 102 Rm: 102 │ │ Email Davis, R. - Rm: 102 ◄── [END] │ └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
Key detail: Make sure each class block contains its "Email [Teacher] - Rm: ..." line!
  1. Log in to your school's PowerSchool student web portal.
  2. Open your Attendance / Schedule View.
  3. Highlight all the course text blocks as shown in the diagram above.
  4. Copy the selection (Ctrl + C on Windows or Cmd + C on Mac).
  5. Paste into the dashboard's text box and click Generate & Save Schedule.
